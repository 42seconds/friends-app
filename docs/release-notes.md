## Install

- **Any phone (recommended):** open **[42seconds.github.io/friends-app](https://42seconds.github.io/friends-app/)**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Works on iPhone and Android, offline too, and updates itself.
- **Android app:** download `andfriends-v*.apk` below, open it, and allow installing from this source.
  If an older &friends APK is installed and Android says *"App not installed"*, uninstall the old one first (each build is signed with its own test key).

## What's new since v1.0.0

**Booking**
- Mix ticket types in one booking (e.g. 2 General + 1 VIP), up to 4 per person
- Early bird tickets with their end date, "only N left", and sold out → join the waitlist for that ticket type
- Past events under *Recently*; past events can't be booked
- Emails in the &FRIENDS design: *Held for you*, *You're in!*, *Quick fix needed*

**Accounts and security**
- Password rules shown as you type, plus confirm password
- Confirm your email with a 6-digit code
- Staff accounts use 2-step verification (password + emailed code)
- Sign in with fingerprint, face or phone PIN

**Everywhere**
- Installable web app for iPhone and Android, with offline support and an app QR code (Settings → Get the app)
- Ticket stub tears off when scanned in at the door; "Back to home" after sending proof
- Access codes: discount and free guest-list codes for staff
- Fixed: the app staying on the splash screen when reopened
- Upgraded to Expo SDK 58; spelling and grammar pass
